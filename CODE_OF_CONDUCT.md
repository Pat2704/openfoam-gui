# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.

In short: everyone is welcome here regardless of experience, background or
nationality. Be civil, assume the other person is acting in good faith, and keep
the discussion on the technical question. Harassment, personal attacks and
deliberate disruption are not tolerated.

CFD has a wide range of expertise levels and this project is aimed partly at
people who are new to OpenFOAM. A question that looks basic is not a nuisance.

To report a problem, use the private reporting channel described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or contact the maintainer through GitHub. Reports
are handled privately, and the maintainer may remove comments, issues or
contributions that do not follow this document.
